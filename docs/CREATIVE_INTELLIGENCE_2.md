# Forge Creative Intelligence 2.0

Creative Intelligence 2.0 is the project-specific art-direction layer above Forge Director and below production execution.

Its purpose is not to make every project more decorative. Its purpose is to make it harder for a technically excellent Forge experience to become creatively generic, derivative, internally inconsistent or recognizably formulaic.

## Operating model

```text
Brief + evidence
    ↓
Director territories
    ↓
Visual Language Generator
    ↓
Creative DNA
    ↓
Art Director
    ↓
Specialist Creative Directors
    ↓
Creative Mutation
    ↓
Creative Memory / Taste / Originality gates
    ↓
Creative Ceiling V2
    ↓
Creative Agent / Construction / Control Plane
    ↓
Visual Director 17-discipline review
```

The runtime, Sequencer, Interaction Graph and Loop Engine are unchanged. Creative Intelligence directs those systems; it does not create a parallel execution engine.

## Creative DNA

Every selected territory produces one project-specific Creative DNA.

The DNA protects:

- north star;
- creative contradiction;
- memory promise;
- signature mechanism;
- composition and negative-space grammar;
- typography personality, scale, measure and motion relationship;
- dominant/support/accent color roles and temperature progression;
- image lens/crop/subject-distance/texture/grading language;
- 3D geometry, material and realism rules;
- motion energy, inertia, acceleration, stillness and signature behavior;
- lighting direction, hardness, temperature, contrast and progression;
- interaction model and restraint;
- sound texture, dynamics, spatiality, silence and interaction behavior;
- mobile-preservation rules;
- project-specific anti-patterns;
- cross-domain precedent transfers.

Creative DNA is not a theme preset. It is synthesized from the brief, selected territory, Director grammar, art bible and evidence.

## Visual Language Generator

Director generates three visual-language worlds for its three territories.

The current language modes are:

- Monumental restraint
- Tactile editorial
- Optical instrument
- Atmospheric cinema
- Graphic system
- Organic sensory
- Kinetic interface
- Raw contrast

A mode is not accepted as a finished style. It is combined with the territory thesis and client truth to produce project-specific rules.

Forge measures pairwise distance across:

- composition;
- typography;
- color;
- image direction;
- material;
- lighting;
- motion;
- interaction;
- sound;
- graphic devices.

Minimum required visual-language distance rises with production tier. Signature and Flagship work can be held when the territories are not materially different enough.

## Art Director

The Art Director converts Creative DNA into a coherent visual system.

It defines:

- visual rule;
- hierarchy rule;
- foreground / dominant / supporting planes;
- negative-space and density rules;
- typography system;
- color system;
- image system;
- material system;
- lighting system;
- motion system;
- interaction system;
- scene-frame direction for every emotional beat;
- craft rules;
- explicit rejection rules.

The Art Director exists because a strong concept can still fail when typography, lighting, materials, imagery and motion appear to come from different projects.

## Specialist Creative Directors

Creative Intelligence produces eight discipline briefs:

- Typography Director
- Camera Director
- Motion Director
- Lighting Director
- Material Director
- Image Director
- Interaction Director
- Sound Director

Each brief contains:

- premise;
- production rules;
- things to avoid;
- mobile interpretation;
- proof required before approval.

These briefs are direction, not new runtimes. They feed the existing Forge systems and production process.

## Creative Mutation Engine

Forge should not spend all production energy improving the first strong idea.

The Mutation Engine creates counterfactual tests such as:

- remove the expected hero;
- make typography the environment;
- withhold motion until the climax;
- make light the signature mechanism;
- begin at macro scale;
- preserve one visual anchor for the entire journey;
- make interaction cause the story;
- reject expected category spectacle;
- let one material behavior control the world;
- reverse the narrative.

Every mutation declares what it preserves, what it changes, the systems affected, originality potential and production risk.

A proposed mutation is **not** stored as institutional memory merely because Forge generated it. The memory command stores a mutation only when it is explicitly selected for the approved creative record.

## Reference Deconstruction 2.0

Reference lessons are deconstructed across:

- composition;
- typography;
- camera;
- motion;
- color;
- image;
- material;
- interaction;
- transitions;
- density;
- narrative;
- signature mechanism;
- mobile.

When the supplied reference lesson does not support a discipline, Forge records it as **not evidenced** rather than inventing a style.

Transfer rule:

> Transfer the causal principle into Forge's own Creative DNA. Never transfer a reference's exact composition, palette, typeface or signature interaction as a bundle.

## Creative Memory

Approved Creative Intelligence can be written into the existing Creative Memory graph.

Memory nodes include:

- Creative Principle / Creative DNA
- Art Direction
- Visual Language
- Creative Mutation
- Camera Grammar
- Motion Grammar
- Typography Grammar
- Color Grammar
- Lighting Grammar
- Material Grammar
- Image Grammar
- Sound Grammar
- Interaction Grammar
- Failure Pattern
- Lesson

Director CLI automatically considers approved memory from **other projects**. Nodes belonging to the current project are excluded from anti-repeat scoring so an existing project does not accuse itself of copying itself.

Creative Memory produces:

- repeated-signal matches;
- useful prior lessons;
- anti-repeat warnings;
- clear / watch / rewrite verdict.

Signature and Flagship work may be held when approved prior memory shows material house-style repetition.

## Layered taste

Taste is intentionally separated into:

1. **Studio taste** — durable quality judgment, weight 0.58.
2. **Operator taste** — secondary personal preference, weight 0.22.
3. **Project taste** — temporary project-specific preference, weight 0.20.

Confidence attenuates every layer.

Taste may influence aesthetic judgment, but it cannot override:

- client facts;
- brand truth;
- evidence;
- accessibility;
- production constraints;
- originality gates.

Use:

```bash
npm run director:preference -- --layer=studio --winner=a --loser=b --reason="Stronger restraint" --restraintVsSpectacle=-0.7
npm run director:preference -- --layer=operator --operator=creative-director --winner=a --loser=b --reason="Better image/type tension"
npm run director:preference -- --layer=project --project=aster-house --winner=b --loser=a --reason="Client needs more warmth"
```

## Creative Ceiling V2

The ceiling is no longer a single high-level number.

It reports:

- concept;
- assets;
- visual language;
- typography;
- camera;
- motion;
- interaction;
- originality;
- lighting;
- materials;
- image direction;
- sound;
- coherence.

It identifies the lowest-scoring bottlenecks and the highest-leverage interventions before more production effort is spent.

The purpose is allocation, not vanity scoring.

## Creative Agent and Construction

Creative Agent consumes the selected Creative DNA, Art Direction, discipline directions and top mutations.

Construction consumes the same intelligence and can fail closed when:

- hierarchy is unresolved;
- critical assets are blocked;
- Signature/Flagship territory distance is insufficient;
- Creative Memory requires a rewrite.

This prevents downstream construction from silently reverting to generic motion archetypes after Director has already defined a stronger visual system.

## Visual Director

Rendered review now covers 17 dimensions:

1. composition
2. typography
3. camera
4. motion
5. continuity
6. brand specificity
7. art direction
8. color
9. lighting
10. material
11. image direction
12. sound
13. originality
14. craft
15. interaction
16. mobile
17. performance

The repair planner remains intentionally narrower than the critic.

Safe presentation, camera and motion symptoms can produce bounded candidates.

Originality, material and sound findings remain diagnostic until Forge has a domain-specific repair that can preserve truth and quality. A generic visual tweak must never masquerade as an originality, material or sonic fix.

## Control Plane

Creative Intelligence remains behind Forge's simple operating model.

Examples:

```text
scene → “this feels generic; develop a more original visual language”
copy → “make the typography more editorial”
media → “direct the image language”
environment → “make this world more alien, but not sci-fi”
```

The Control Plane routes these contextual intents to Creative Agent with the current selection and scene context.

The operator does not need to choose Art Director, Lighting Director, Material Director or Mutation Engine manually.

## Verification

Creative Intelligence is covered by:

- Director Intelligence audit across 10 project types × 4 tiers;
- three unique visual-language worlds per run;
- pairwise territory-distance matrix;
- Art Director coverage of every emotional beat;
- all eight discipline directors;
- mutation-depth requirements;
- 13-dimension Creative Ceiling V2;
- 17-dimension rendered-review contract;
- Control Plane creative-intent tests;
- memory, taste and reference-deconstruction tests.

## Boundaries

Creative Intelligence 2.0 improves the quality of direction and the rigor of creative choices.

It does **not** claim that deterministic text generation alone proves award-winning visual output.

Production still requires:

- strong source assets;
- rendered candidate review;
- human creative judgment at required gates;
- real-device validation;
- project-specific photography/3D/material/audio craft where the concept depends on them.

The system is successful when it produces better creative decisions, broader genuine alternatives, stronger coherence and fewer repeated house solutions—not when it merely produces more instructions.
