# HELIOT — Observatory for the unseen

The first delivery overexposed weak product geometry and repeated a single editorial arrangement. This rebuild changes the subject to an original light observatory: an inhabited annulus in a volcanic landscape. The visitor moves from landscape to material, dismantles its optical assembly, directs light, and returns to the landscape with a saved field study.

## Architecture and assets

One R3F Canvas; Forge CinematicFrame, camera sampler, ProductRig, progress channel, adaptive quality, lifecycle and procedural contour generator. An isolated image-plane shader supplies restrained spatial photography and circular occlusion within that same Canvas. No independent animation ticker. Scroll-derived framing is reversible. A local conceptual light-lab state bridges semantic controls and the visual iris. DOM content remains available without JavaScript/WebGL; mobile uses dedicated text safe areas and image crops.

Original generated landscape and macro art are local optimized assets, with provenance. No weak placeholder geometry presented as photoreal. The 3D annulus is deliberately a technical maquette, with named bronze components, radial fins and structural rings. Tone changes distinguish landscape, material, technical drawing, interaction and atmospheric resolution.
