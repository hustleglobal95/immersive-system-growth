# Immersive Project Brief

## Goal
What should the visitor understand, feel and do?

## Primary conversion
One action only.

## Audience
Who is this experience for?

## Art direction
Materials, lighting, references, typography, realism level and prohibited motifs.

## Persistent visual anchor
Which object or environment element carries continuity across scenes?

## Required scenes
List moments, not webpage sections.

## Available assets
Use exact filenames and paths.

## Missing assets
List what must be produced before implementation.

## Target devices
Desktop, tablet, mobile and any minimum hardware assumptions.

## Performance constraints
Maximum initial payload, model budgets and required fallback behavior.
