# David Weekley Homes: FORGE Redesign Concept

## Objective

Create a complete cinematic home-discovery experience using only FORGE systems and assets. Preserve the practical job of the public website while replacing the disconnected module stack with one guided spatial narrative.

## Primary action

Schedule a personal tour.

## Secondary action

Explore homes by market.

## Public facts used

- Founded in Houston in 1976
- One of the largest privately held home builders in America
- Operations across 13 states and 19 markets
- Brand promise centered on Design, Choice, and Service
- Purpose: Building Dreams, Enhancing Lives

## Scope

This branch is an independent redesign concept. It uses no proprietary photography or unverified inventory data. Search actions intentionally return visitors to the public David Weekley Homes market experience.

## Journey

1. Arrival: search by market without losing the emotional opening.
2. Place: establish that choosing a home begins with choosing a life and location.
3. Community: connect landscape, neighborhood, and architecture.
4. Threshold: cross physically into the home.
5. Living: slow the experience and show everyday spatial quality.
6. Choice: explain personalization through materials and decisions.
7. Difference: resolve Design, Choice, and Service into credible brand proof.
8. Welcome: present the tour action with calm confidence.
