# NOCTERRA Residence 01

## Creative premise

NOCTERRA is a fictional waterfront residence brand built around one idea: the visitor should never feel that they are scrolling through independent website sections. They should feel that one camera operator is guiding them through a place.

The experience begins with architecture, water and an automotive object sharing one frame. The car becomes the first subject, then gives the frame back to the residence. The camera crosses the threshold, slows inside, moves into material detail, returns to the terrace and resolves on the horizon before presenting the final inquiry action.

## Direction rules

- Preserve one continuous spatial world wherever possible.
- Use camera travel to change subject rather than page cuts.
- Keep copy short and subordinate to imagery.
- Use close shots to create contrast before major reveals.
- Let architecture hide transitions when possible.
- Treat the waterfront reveal as the visual climax.
- Keep the final CTA inside the cinematic composition.
- Desktop and mobile use separate authored camera states.
- Reduced motion must remain supported.
- Reference assets are placeholders for production-grade art, not final client artwork.

## Delivery target

The project should remain a normal validated Forge client bundle. Camera tracks may later be regenerated through the Camera Director and Global Sequence Director, but the final result must remain editable and deterministic.
