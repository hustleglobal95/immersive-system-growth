# TIC TOC

## Brand thesis

Tic Toc treats time as architecture. The debut piece, No. 01 Obsidian Tourbillon, is designed as a dark mechanical object revealed through light, scale, soundless tension, and deliberate motion.

## Visual system

- Obsidian: `#070808`
- Graphite: `#171919`
- Champagne gold: `#C9A86A`
- Bone: `#F3EEE5`
- Midnight: `#07141B`
- Display: Didot or Bodoni style high contrast serif
- Interface: Inter or a neutral modern grotesk

## Art direction

Black DLC titanium, warm mechanical highlights, deep blue interiors, hard-edged pools of light, and restrained reflections. Product imagery remains isolated and sculptural. Motion is slow, weighted, and directly connected to scroll or intent.

## Voice

Precise, assured, sparse. Short declarative phrases. No hype language. The experience sells rarity through control and detail.
