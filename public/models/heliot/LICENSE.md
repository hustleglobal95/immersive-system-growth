# Original HELIOT geometry

These GLBs are generated entirely by `scripts/generate-heliot.mjs` from original project geometry. They contain no imported third-party model or texture assets. Distributed under this repository's MIT license. HELIOT is imagined architecture, not a reproduction of an existing building or manufacturer's instrument.

The two accompanying WebP plates were generated for this project with the built-in image-generation tool. Their prompts and provenance are recorded in `docs/HELIOT_ART_DIRECTION.md`. They do not depict a real commissioned building.
